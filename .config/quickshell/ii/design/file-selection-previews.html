Selection, without a box

Same rows, same list, five ways to say “this is the one”. The scroll lane stays on the left in every study, so each marker is drawn inside the row — never across the pane edge.

Chosen: 02’s shape, with the blue accent replaced by neutral grey — a wash at 7.5% white and a 2 px rail at 70% white. The live app draws that in the file list, the tiles, the Quick access rows and the drive rows, all inset past the 8 px scroll lane.

A 1 px stroke round the whole row. Finds the row, but reads as a border sitting over the list — and its left edge lands in the scroll lane.
