The reason they are not at the edge is not the spacing. The row is [spacer][skip][play][skip][spacer] where both spacers are Layout.fillWidth: true. Two fill-width items share ALL the leftover width equally, so the two skips are pushed together into a cluster in the middle of the card and the empty space lands outside them. The comment above that code says “skip back hard left, play dead centre, skip forward hard right” — the code does the opposite, and no value of spacing changes that. The real fix is to drop the spacers and let each of the three buttons centre itself in its own third.
