Left sidebar tabs & Music — design previews

Two components, six options each, drawn at the sidebar's real width (480px, Appearance.sizes.sidebarWidth). Variant A is what's on screen right now, included so you can compare against it. Colours come from Appearance.colors.* in the real build, so a chosen layout follows your live theme — the swatches here are just the shell's current Material 3 dark scheme.
