The desktop pill at the bottom of the screen (DesktopIndicator.qml) is Bar.Workspaces in compact metrics: a row of 11×3 lines, 21px cells, 8px apart, inside a 22px capsule. Today the active desktop is the same line in accent colour — switching desktops teleports that colour with no animation at all.

Five ways to animate the handover. Each one loops on its own; use the buttons to drive it by hand, or Replay to fire a single switch. Every stage shows the real pill at 1×, with a 4× inset so 3px marks are actually judgeable.
