Nine chimes, one gesture: three notes, 80 ms apart, −6 / 0 / −4 dB. The desktop plays warm, A4 / E5 / B5. The rest are here so it can be changed on another day.

Tone, not timing: the gesture is fixed at three notes, 80 ms apart, −6 / 0 / −4 dB, peak −3 dBFS. What moves is the voice: the warm takes are a soft sine with the contact tick removed entirely (3.5–4 ms attack, roll-off at 4.2 or 3.6 kHz), the marimba keeps odd partials (1, 3, 5) on a short body, the bell uses inharmonic ones (2.76, 5.4), the pluck is a Karplus-Strong string, and the deep wood is the shipped character at G3–A4 — a register below every low take tried so far.
Low arch: the shipped take climbs 587 / 1077 / 1976 Hz, 48 ms apart, which is why its top two steps fuse and why it reads as a signal rather than as news. The two below keep every part of that design — 2–3 ms attack, contact tick 55 % quieter and lower, upper partials pulled back, two-pole roll-off at 5.5 kHz, −6 / 0 / −4 dB arch, peak −3 dBFS — and move only the notes (A4–B5 down to C4–D5) and the spacing (80 ms, so each note has room).
Damping is the edges, not the gesture: the three takes below are the even-step B6 and D7 with a 2–3 ms attack instead of 0.8, a contact tick 55 % quieter and lower, the 2nd and 3rd partials pulled back, and a two-pole roll-off above 5.5 kHz (3.8 kHz on the last one). Notes, levels, 48 ms spacing and 172 ms length are untouched.
Even steps: the ramp was +7 semitones and then +14 — it accelerated, which is what makes the last step feel like a jump rather than the top of a climb. The top three put an equal interval between every pair (10.5, 12, 12 semitones), so the gesture rises at one rate, and the level arch and spacing are still `notify-arch3-soft`'s.
Only the last note moved: the three below are `notify-arch3-soft` exactly — same three ticks, same −6 / 0 / −4 dB arch, same 48 ms spacing — with the third note raised from 1318 Hz to 1760, 1976 or 2349 Hz. A fourth, a fifth, or the whole top octave.
Three steps, not two: the arch used to put its first note at −16 dB, which is quiet enough that only the loud middle and the top arrived — two steps. In the three takes below it the spread is 6, 9 or 12 dB instead, so the first note is a real step, while the level still swells in the middle and eases at the top.
Only the notes moved: the five arch takes are the same ticks as before — same partials, same contact transients, same 40 ms spacing, same 155 ms, peak −3 dBFS, same −16 / 0 / −12 dB arch across the levels. The only difference between them is which three notes are played, so the gesture starts low and finishes on its highest note. The level-shaped steps below are untouched for comparison.
