Obsidianpure black

The window is literally #000000. Cards float one or two steps off black with hairline borders instead of shadows. The accent is white — selected controls invert, so a "checked" chip is a white pill with black text. Squared geometry, small radii, maximum contrast between chrome and content. Reads like a high-end terminal or a car infotainment screen.

  • window #000
  • cards #0e0e0e
  • accent = white
  • radius 5–6px
  • hairline borders, no shadows
  • inverted selected state

Graphitecharcoal + silver

Soft charcoal surfaces (#181a1d content, #1e2125 cards) with clearly visible #282c32 hairlines. The accent is silver, not white — selected states are a raised grey rather than a full inversion. Larger radii, more breathing room, a real elevation feel. This is the closest relative to what you have now, minus the green.

  • window #15171a
  • cards #1e2125
  • accent = silver #cfd4da
  • radius 10–12px
  • visible borders + soft shadow
  • raised-grey selected state

Carbonterminal / monospace

The most extreme of the four. Backgrounds sit at #0a0a0b, borders are barely-there, and the accent is a mid grey (#8a8a92) so nothing ever screams. Everything is monospace, labels are wide-tracked uppercase, 2px corners, tighter rows. It reads like a professional monitoring console — dense, quiet, and completely colourless.

  • window #0a0a0b
  • cards #111113
  • accent = grey #8a8a92
  • radius 2px
  • monospace throughout
  • dense rows, wide-tracked labels

Titaniumlayered greys + glass

Translucent white overlays instead of flat fills: cards are rgba(255,255,255,.045) over a charcoal-to-near-black vertical gradient, so they pick up the wallpaper like frosted glass. Accent is near-white, radii are large (14–16px), spacing is generous, and the rail uses a soft translucent highlight. The "premium laptop hardware" option — the Grafizum look, but neutral.

  • gradient window #1b1d21 → #121417
  • translucent cards
  • accent = near-white #eef0f3
  • radius 14–16px
  • roomy spacing
  • inner highlight edges
