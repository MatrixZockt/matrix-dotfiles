Trading tab — content display designs

A new tab for the top-middle NowBar island, and the expanded NowBarPanel card it pops out. The subject is today's red-folder economic calendar — the high-impact releases (rate decisions, CPI, NFP, FOMC) that move price — plus a few glanceable market readouts. Every mock below is drawn at the shell's own palette and type scale.

Red folder, defined. ForexFactory-style impact tiers: red = high impact, orange = medium, yellow = low, grey = holiday. The tab is built around the red tier and uses the other tiers only as quiet context. Each event carries time · currency · name · forecast · previous · actual.
Where the data comes from (for the build step, not this preview). An economic-calendar JSON feed polled on a timer — the community ForexFactory mirror, Trading Economics, Finnhub or FMP all serve the same shape — plus a quote feed for the watchlist, sessions and sparklines. The designs below assume one refresh function per panel and a 1-minute cadence; nothing here needs a websocket.

A · island rest state (≈360 px wide, 40 px tall)

What the tab shows when it is the resting view and the card is closed. Two ideas: count down to the next red event, or carry a live ticker.

A1 · Next-event countdown

island: [folder] CURRENCY · name · MM:SS

Why: the single most useful number a trader wants at a glance is “how long until the next red release”. No reading required, and it survives a 360 px island because the name just ellipsises. The sparkline + ticks on the right keep the bar alive between events.

A2 · Live ticker + session chip

island: scrolling instrument · px · Δ% · session dot
DXY 107.42 ▲ 0.31%
LDN open

Why: reads like a mini Bloomberg strip. Cycles the watchlist. Pairs naturally with the auto-rotation logic the island already has (timer > sound > rest); trading would sit alongside, and the session chip answers “which desk is awake”.

Once you pick, the build is: a TradingService (calendar + quotes, one refresh timer), a TradingView for the island, a tab in NowBarPanel, and a fifth mode in NowBar.qml's ring. Nothing here needs a new dependency — sparklines and the session arc are plain Shape/Canvas.
